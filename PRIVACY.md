# Privacy Policy — eBay Seller Feedback Filter

_Last updated: 2026-08-17_

eBay Seller Feedback Filter ("the extension") is built to respect your privacy.
It does **not** collect, transmit, sell, or share any personal or browsing data.

## What the extension accesses
- It reads the contents of eBay **search-results pages locally, in your
  browser**, to hide listings and highlight deals according to your settings.
  This processing happens entirely on your device.

## What it stores
- Your settings (rating thresholds and the filter/best-deal toggles) are saved
  with Chrome's `storage.sync` API so they persist and follow your signed-in
  Chrome profile. This data stays within your own Chrome account and is never
  sent to the developer or any third party.

## What it does NOT do
- No analytics, tracking, cookies, or fingerprinting.
- No network requests of any kind — the extension makes zero outbound calls.
- No collection of personal information, purchase history, or account data.

## Permissions
- `storage` — to save your settings.
- Host access to eBay domains — to run the filtering script on eBay search
  pages (the extension's single purpose).

## Changes
Material changes to this policy will be reflected in this file with an updated
date.
